Accounts with Balance Greater than 50,000

Filters all bank accounts where the available balance exceeds ₹50,000 using WHERE clause. 

SELECT 
    account_id,
    customer_id,
    account_type,
    balance,
    opening_date,
    account_status
FROM accounts
WHERE balance > 50000.00
ORDER BY balance DESC;
